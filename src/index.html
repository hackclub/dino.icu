welcome to dino.icu

loading dinos from hackclub/dinosaurs

what's dino.icu?

dino.icu is a free subdomain service that hack clubbers - host your own personal site or project on the dino.icu domain!

where do these come from?

every dino was drawn by someone in the hack club community and added to hackclub/dinosaurs on github.

how do i add my own dino?

we have a snazzy guide at hack.af/make-dino, go check it out to make your own :)

how can i get in touch?

make a github issue! or, if you're in the hack club slack, you can also ask in the #hq-engineering channel!
